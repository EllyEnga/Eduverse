About Eduverse

Empowering Zambian Education

We're building the future of learning in Zambia — one student, one teacher, one school at a time.

Our Mission

To make quality education accessible to every Zambian learner, regardless of location or background. We connect students with skilled teachers, provide structured learning paths, and create a supportive community.

  • Accessible education for all
  • Empowering teachers to share knowledge
  • Building a community of lifelong learners
  • Supporting Zambian curriculum

What We Offer

Eduverse is designed specifically for the Zambian education system. Whether you're a primary school pupil, a secondary school student, or a teacher, we have the tools you need.

  • Subjects aligned with Zambian curriculum
  • Teacher verification and support
  • Parent and community involvement
  • Affordable and accessible learning

Help & FAQ

How do I enroll in a course?

Go to Courses, open any course, and click "Enroll". Your request will be sent to the admin for approval. For paid courses, admin will verify payment before approving.

How are quizzes graded?

Quizzes are scored instantly — you'll see which answers were correct right away.

How do I become a teacher?

Click "Join as Teacher" on the home page, fill in the application form. When approved, you'll receive a teacher code via email.

How do I get a certificate?

Complete all lessons in a course. A certificate will be automatically generated and you can download it as PDF.

How do I record pupil results?

Go to the Results page, select a course, and add pupil names with their grades.
